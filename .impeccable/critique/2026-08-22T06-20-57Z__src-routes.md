## Design Specificity Verdict

The adding-machine/VFD concept is executed with rare discipline — phosphor glow, engraved faceplate labels, segmented progress bars, flash keyframe on save, block-glyph identity, quick-add as paper tape. An unrelated product cannot wear this skin. But the composition is generic: standard sidebar + topbar + FAB + bottom-nav shell. Specificity lives in surface treatment, not structure.

## Overall Impression

The concept is load-bearing and rare. The VFD metaphor earns its place through interaction rather than decoration. Two P0 issues undermine the foundation: the hero readout can compute wrong numbers, and the .surface class was removed while still in use by 12 components.

## What's Working

1. The concept is load-bearing, not decorative — VFD glow, tape quick-add, faceplate labels, flash-on-keypress are woven into interaction.
2. Recognition-over-recall is genuinely engineered — payee autocomplete, auto-learned tags, draft persistence, one-click repeat with precise undo.
3. Accessibility scaffolding exceeds the norm — focus traps, live regions, prefers-reduced-motion, computed contrast in token comments.

## Priority Issues

P0 — Hero readout can be wrong: transactions.load({ limit: 5 }) computes monthFlow from 5 rows regardless of date. Fix: month-bounded query.
P0 — Phantom .surface class: used in 12 components, defined nowhere after polish pass. Cards render with no background/border. Fix: restore definition.
P1 — Placeholder text fails WCAG AA: text-dim/60 and text-dim/70 drop to 2.5-3.0:1. Fix: use full-opacity --dim.
P1 — Missing ARIA on segmented controls and quick-add input: no radiogroup, no aria-pressed, no aria-label. Fix: add ARIA attributes.
P1 — State coverage gaps: all main pages have loading/error in stores but none read them. Fix: add loading/error UI.
