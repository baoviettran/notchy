## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Notchy's world is genuinely authored. `.figures`/`.plate`/`.figures-glow`, the VFD net-position readout, the quick-add tape strip, the currency chip stamped "VN"/"US", and the debt-free lamp could belong to no other fintech. The metaphor is carried hardest exactly where DESIGN.md promises (money surfaces) and abandoned where workhorse surfaces may go quiet — but two chrome zones break the spell: the Sidebar/BottomNav stroke icons (Sidebar.svelte:33-42) and TopBar's magnifier-SVG search are stock SaaS furniture, and chart legend swatches read generic. Most damning: the signature `.animate-flash` "keypress" moment defined in app.css:142 never fires on any actual save — it exists only on StartupProgress and RecoveryScreen.

**Deterministic scan**: CLI detector returned zero findings across both scans (src/routes and src/lib/components). Browser visualization on a live dev server found real issues on /quick-add that static rules missed: hardcoded 11px text (quick-add/+page.svelte:195,233,238,243) and a flat type hierarchy (11/15/16px, ratio 1.5:1). Two browser findings were verified false positives by reading source: `dark-glow` is the documented dark-only `.figures-glow` bloom (app.css:94-105), and `cream-palette` is the designed light-theme `--ink` token (app.css:49). Caveat: under direct URL load, /transactions and /debts rendered identically to / (likely SPA-fallback shell render), so per-route browser evidence for those routes is unreliable.

**Visual overlays**: Injection succeeded in headless Chromium but no user-facing [Human] tab exists in this session — no user-visible overlay is available. Console evidence was captured programmatically instead.

## Overall Impression

This is a rare thing: a local finance app with an actual design POV, executed with measured contrast discipline and consequence-framed destructive flows. The gaps are not aesthetic but dramatic — the machine has a signature gesture it never performs at its most-used moment, saves vanish without registering, and the two emotional milestones (debt-free vs goal-complete) get wildly different investment. Fix the unwired moments before touching any layout.

## What's Working

1. **Quick-add window** (quick-add/+page.svelte) — a pure adding-machine tape: mono-only, `▮` mark, ESC/SAVE plates, live payee echo, refocus re-resolving the default account, honest database_update_required state.
2. **Measured color discipline** — corrected --line values with contrast math preserved as history in app.css; palette.ts derives chart ramps purely from tokens so series flip with theme.
3. **Write-off consequence framing** (debts/+page.svelte:152-165) — context sentence, live "Remaining after this" preview flipping to phosphor at zero. The pattern every other confirm should copy.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] The signature moment is unwired.** DESIGN.md §5 defines `.animate-flash` as the machine registering a keypress on save; grep shows it fires only on startup/recovery screens. Neither TransactionForm.save nor quick-add's submit flickers.
   Why: the app's identity gesture is absent from its highest-frequency action; the world reads as theme, not machine.
   Fix: flash the saved amount in the confirmation toast; one-frame bright flash before hideWindow() in quick-add/+page.svelte:123.
   Suggested command: `$impeccable animate`
2. **[P1] Toasts are invisible to screen readers and expire under your finger.** Toast.svelte:15 lacks role="status"/aria-live; the 5s timer kills undo with no pause-on-hover/focus. Every save/delete confirmation routes through this.
   Fix: role="status" aria-live="polite"; pause timer on pointerenter/focusin.
   Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`
3. **[P1] Danger button fails AA contrast.** Button.svelte:17 danger uses bg-debit text-ledger ≈ 2.5:1 in dark (needs 4.5); text-ink would pass ≈ 4.7:1. This is the button on every irreversible confirm.
   Fix: swap danger label color to ink like primary.
   Suggested command: `$impeccable polish`
4. **[P2] The /transactions filter wall.** Four w-44 selects permanently visible above the list; mobile puts them top-of-scroll as a 2×2 dropdown grid.
   Fix: collapse behind a single Filter toggle with active-count badge; keep SQL filtering unchanged.
   Suggested command: `$impeccable distill`
5. **[P2] Loading states regress the metaphor — and quick-add's plates fail size discipline.** Every route blanks to centered dim loading text while Skeleton sits unwired; detector confirmed 11px hardcoded text and flat hierarchy on quick-add.
   Fix: wire Skeleton as advancing ▮▯▯▯ cells; lift quick-add micro-labels to the .plate standard or larger where they carry body meaning.
   Suggested command: `$impeccable layout`

Also flagged: goal completion gets no moment while debt-free gets a lamp (emotional inconsistency); goals_delete_confirm_body claims irreversibility without numeric restatement (violates the app's own Don't); large-discrepancy dialog interpolates the raw unparsed balance string (accounts/[id]/+page.svelte:153).

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user)**: Only n and / shortcuts exist (+layout.svelte:85-86); the quick-add window — the power tool — has no shortcut, menu item, or hint anywhere in the main window. Prev/next pagination only, PAGE_SIZE = 50 hidden constant, no jump/load-more. Budget inline edit has no autofocus and parse errors fire as a toast far from the field.

**Sam (a11y)**: Silent toasts and AA-failing danger button (issues 2–3); ContextMenu triggers at 32×32px below the 44px floor; ConfirmDialog focuses the panel container, not a control (ConfirmDialog.svelte:28), so SR users hear the title into silence; .plate at 11px uppercase 0.18em tracking with Vietnamese diacritics ("TÀI SẢN THUẦN") — a risk DESIGN.md §7 itself names, unmitigated.

**Casey (mobile one-handed)**: Content pads pb-24 (96px) but FAB occupies 80–136px from bottom (FAB.svelte:11) — last list row's menu trigger can sit under the FAB at scroll end on /transactions. Filter wall at top of long scroll maximally hostile one-handed. More-sheet grid labels at text-xs under w-6 icons in grid-cols-4 are tight targets on 320px screens.

## Minor Observations

- FAB casts a phosphor glow shadow in both themes — violates the "no glow in light mode" Don't (FAB.svelte:13).
- Income amounts on accounts/[id] rows get no `+` prefix while expenses get `−` (:102) — the glyph grammar halves itself.
- ConfirmDialog.danger defaults true (:6) — future non-destructive confirms will be scary by accident.
- Import button floats alone in its own action row (transactions/:137-139).
- ErrorState's exclamation-circle SVG isn't in the glyph table — first arbitrary icon in the vocabulary.
- Finishing onboarding drops you on the dashboard with no arrival moment.
- Reconcile styled as danger frames a corrective act as destructive.

## Questions to Consider

- If glow is reserved for money, why does the FAB wear a permanent phosphor halo in light mode while the net-position hero loses its bloom on paper?
- Is quick-add the real product? Should the main-window FAB open that tape as an overlay instead of the 7-field form?
- When the machine reconciles, shouldn't the adjustment print onto the tape as a visible, reversible entry rather than surfacing only as a toast?
