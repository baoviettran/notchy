## Design Specificity Verdict

Authored, not templated. The "Adding Machine" system (src/app.css) — warm near-black casing, amber-phosphor VFD figures in IBM Plex Mono, oxblood debits, engraved .plate labels, quick-add as calculator tape — could not be lifted into a generic fintech app without losing meaning. Weakness: the metaphor is carried beautifully in display surfaces (dashboard, quick-add) but thins out in workhorse surfaces (Transactions). docs/DESIGN.md describes a completely different visual world (zinc/emerald Tailwind, Lucide icons, mobile tab bar spec) than what is built — misinformation for contributors.

Deterministic scan: detect.mjs --json src/routes exited clean with zero findings. Remaining issues are structural/UX-level.

## Overall Impression

An app with an actual point of view. The problem is distribution, not direction: metaphor and care are concentrated in showcase surfaces while Transactions got the least design, and protection is calibrated to how scary the word delete sounds rather than severity of loss. Biggest opportunity: bring the workbench up to the standard quick-add already set.

## What's Working

1. Token system in src/app.css: six semantic RGB triplets driving Tailwind alpha modifiers, documented contrast corrections (#807763 chosen because #2A2419 measured 1.15:1), prefers-reduced-motion kills animation and glow.
2. Quick Add as a product decision: separate Tauri window, one focused input, localized parse grammar (50k, 1.5tr, 100+50), careful multi-window DB lifecycle.
3. Honest destructive-action engineering: impact-counted deletes, reconcile discrepancy guard, exact-captured-ID undo (FrequentTransactions.svelte:30-34).

## Priority Issues

[P1] Transactions has no filtering or sorting (src/routes/transactions/+page.svelte)
Why: highest-frequency retrieval task is search-or-scroll through 50-row pages; trust erodes past ~200 rows.
Fix: one filter chip row (kind · account · tag · month) feeding existing load({query}) params; keep search orthogonal.
Command: $impeccable shape

[P1] Hover-gated actions exclude keyboard and touch users (debts/+page.svelte:92,119; goals/+page.svelte:74-75)
Why: `pointer-fine:opacity-0 pointer-fine:group-hover:opacity-100` may not be registered Tailwind variants — reveal possibly inert; never triggers on focus. Pay/Write-off/Delete are the purpose of those screens.
Fix: group-hover:opacity-100 group-focus-within:opacity-100 with visible base state on coarse pointers; audit whether classes generate CSS.
Command: $impeccable audit

[P2] Reports IA redundancy and overload (reports/+page.svelte:53-66 + ReportsNav.svelte)
Why: seven peer tabs plus four link cards duplicating four of them — 11 navigation targets, two competing metaphors.
Fix: ReportsNav as single mechanism, delete card grid, fold YoY into Compare.
Command: $impeccable distill

[P2] Form errors are not field-level (TransactionForm.svelte:160; same pattern Debts/Budget modals)
Why: one red sentence forces full field re-scan on a 7-field form; fails WCAG 3.3.1 without aria-describedby. Input.svelte already has an unused `error` prop.
Fix: route parse/validation failures into owning Input's error prop.
Command: $impeccable harden

[P2] Write-off lacks consequence framing (debts/+page.svelte:131-141)
Why: irreversibly mutates balances (no undo) yet identical modal shell to routine payment.
Fix: show "Remaining balance: X → 0", distinguish partial vs full, danger-variant confirm for full-balance case.
Command: $impeccable clarify
