## Design Specificity Verdict

Authored, not themed. The Adding Machine world survives contact with real screens: VFD net-position readout whose negatives refuse the glow on purpose (+page.svelte:95-107); reports print as statements with dotted leaders, dashed subtotal seam, double-ruled total (TapeLine.svelte); segmented hardware budget meter; quick-add chrome engraved with ▮ ESC ⏎. Metaphor carries information: sign always glyph-paired, over-budget flips to oxblood and prints ▲.

Periphery thins: reports pages abandon primitives (raw select/input type=month/input number with bg-tape instead of casing fill); ReportsNav's seven wrapping web-tabs are not something the machine would do.

## Overall Impression

Center of gravity — dashboard, statement reports, quick-add — is genuinely designed and detector-clean for the first time. Remaining work is the long tail: interaction paths that betray the app's own commitments (focus-reveal, field-level errors) and one structural gap (no read path for transactions). Biggest opportunity: make the periphery as authored as the core.

## Strengths

- Money as accessibility architecture: single figure chokepoint, compact display + sr-only full precision, glyph-paired tone.
- Statement layout: TapeLine dotted leaders/double rule; decorative stacked bar aria-hidden because ruled lines carry data.
- Peak-end discipline: write-off previews remaining balance and turns phosphor at zero; deletion undo pauses countdown on hover.
